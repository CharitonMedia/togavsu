What Doc Tender does

Every file that lands in a matter is processed as it arrives: text and Markdown are read as they are; Word documents are converted and checked against their own word and page counts; spreadsheets become one table per sheet; PDFs are read page by page (a text layer is checked against an OCR read, a scanned page against a second read by Tesseract); photos are described and grouped into documents once the matter has been quiet; audio and video get timestamped transcripts, and video adds what is on screen. A file on a closed matter is listed as Not processed (matter closed) and costs nothing until the matter reopens.

How names are made

Each file gets one name from the naming pattern, lowercase with hyphens, for example smith-2026-0042-motion-to-withdraw-2026-10-15.docx. A clash in the matter adds -2, -3. Status is a tag beside the name, never part of it. A name, category, matter or grouping a person sets is locked and never changed by Doc Tender.

The review queue

Only exceptions reach the queue: low confidence, models that disagree, a matter that can't be matched, an extraction that failed after its retries, a password-protected, damaged or unsupported file, or a provider down for an hour. Each item offers Accept, Correct, Assign matter, Retry and Keep as is. The file stays available with its flag the whole time.

Where its settings live

The Doc Tender tab holds the naming pattern, categories, review confidence, quiet time, retry schedule, recipients, the daily summary and spend. Its models are on the AI tab.
